## 5. Projects
Portfolio reports **10+ ML/AI projects built** and **5+ deployed ML projects**. These are portfolio-level totals; the individual examples below are selected highlights, not a complete inventory.

### Freight Rate Predictor
Predicts a posted freight rate from shipment details such as distance, weight, equipment, and date. Compared Linear Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost; built a FastAPI backend with Pydantic validation, Streamlit interface, and Dockerized API.
- **Reported assessment metrics:** Linear Regression R² ≈ 0.8651, MAE ≈ 140.65, RMSE ≈ 537.22; Random Forest MAE ≈ 129.68; XGBoost MAE ≈ 123.32, R² ≈ 0.8610.
- Demo: https://freight-rate-predictor.streamlit.app/
- Code: https://github.com/tyhan-data/Freight-Rate-Predictor
- Docker Hub: https://hub.docker.com/r/tyhan55/freight-rate-api

### NewsCortex AI
Classifies news articles into World, Sports, Business, and Sci/Tech using TF-IDF (10,000 unigram/bigram features) and a TensorFlow/Keras neural network. Includes FastAPI probability-based predictions and a Streamlit app; Dockerized.
- **Reported result:** About 91% test accuracy on 120K+ AG News articles.
- Demo: https://newscortex-mat.streamlit.app/
- Code: https://github.com/tyhan-data/news-topic-classification-ann
- Docker Hub: https://hub.docker.com/r/tyhan55/newscortex-api

### Heart Disease Predictor
A classification model that estimates an outcome/risk according to its training target and returns a prediction with probability through FastAPI. Uses Pydantic validation and a Streamlit client; packaged in Docker.
- Demo: https://heart-disease-predictor-mat.streamlit.app/
- Code: https://github.com/tyhan-data/heart-disease-predictor
- Docker Hub: https://hub.docker.com/r/tyhan55/heart-api
- **Safety note:** An educational prediction tool, not medical diagnosis.

### CineMatch AI
